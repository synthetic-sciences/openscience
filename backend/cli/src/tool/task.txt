Delegate one bounded independent unit only when parallel work materially improves the result.

Profiles:
{agents}

Choose by work: `explore` finds evidence, `execute` implements or computes, and `review` checks a
high-cost observable result. Lazy skills provide domain expertise; do not invent personas.

- Default to no children. Normal permits two calls per turn; Ultra four; continuations count.
- Children cannot call Task. The lead owns synthesis and verification.
- Do not delegate sequential work, small known-file edits, ceremonial review, or duplicate retrieval.
- Task blocks the lead. Issue useful independent calls together; optional failure must not block a
  usable result.
- Continue a returned `session_id` only for missing work. Otherwise include objective, exact inputs
  and references, relevant context, assumptions, exclusions, allowed actions, output, and validation.
- A child returns a bounded handoff record; its full transcript remains in `session_id`. Preserve
  decisive evidence and do not redo its phase.
