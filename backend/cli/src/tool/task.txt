Delegate one bounded independent unit when parallel work materially improves the result.

Profiles:
{agents}

Use `explore` for evidence, `execute` for implementation or computation, and `review` to check an
observable result. Skills add domain expertise; do not invent personas.

- Default to no children. Normal permits two calls per turn; Ultra four, including continuations.
- Children cannot call Task. The lead owns synthesis and verification.
- Do not delegate sequential work, small edits, ceremonial review, or duplicate retrieval.
- Task blocks the lead. Issue independent calls together; optional failure must not block the result.
- Continue a `session_id` only for missing work. Otherwise include objective, inputs, references,
  context, assumptions, exclusions, allowed actions, output, and validation.
- A child returns a bounded handoff; its transcript remains in `session_id`. Preserve decisive
  evidence and do not redo its phase.
