Delegate one independent unit when parallel work materially improves the result.

Profiles:
{agents}

Choose by work: `explore` finds evidence and `execute` implements or computes. Lazy skills provide
domain expertise; do not invent personas.

- Use as many independent workers as materially useful. Delegation posture guides how readily to
  delegate; it is not a quota. Runtime capacity may queue work.
- Children may call Task for genuinely independent work within the same permission boundary. The
  lead still owns synthesis and verification.
- Do not delegate sequential work, small edits, ceremonial review, or duplicate retrieval.
- Task returns when that child hands off. Issue independent calls together; optional failure must
  not block the result.
- Continue a `session_id` only for missing work. Otherwise include objective, inputs, references,
  context, assumptions, exclusions, allowed actions, output, and validation.
- A child returns a decision-ready handoff; its transcript remains in `session_id`. Preserve decisive
  evidence and do not redo its phase.
