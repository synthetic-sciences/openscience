Use this tool only when the active independence posture allows pausing for user input. Set `reason` precisely:
- `planning`: collaborate on an approach or preference (Interactive only)
- `consequential`: the choice materially changes scope, outcome, cost, or risk (Interactive and Balanced)
- `missing_authority`: required authority or input is unavailable and progress is blocked (all modes)

Usage notes:
- When `custom` is enabled (default), a "Type your own answer" option is added automatically; don't include "Other" or catch-all options
- Answers are returned as arrays of labels; set `multiple: true` to allow selecting more than one
- Planning and consequential questions must provide at least two concrete choices, put the recommended choice first, and add "(Recommended)" at the end of its label
- In Independent mode, planning and consequential decisions take the recommended safe path and continue; do not relabel a preference as missing authority. Such a call emitted anyway resolves to its first recommended option without UI, and the assumption is recorded in the tool trace.
