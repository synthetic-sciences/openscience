OpenScience never requires an account. Local models, your own provider keys, eligible ChatGPT/Codex access, scientific tools, Python/R, and direct downloads continue to work without a plan. Connecting a **Synthetic Sciences account** adds the optional managed Gateway.

## What connecting adds

- **Managed model credits.** Use supported models without configuring a provider key. Only Gateway-served calls use managed credits.
- **Managed research capacity.** Ace includes a generous research quota. Ace+ includes 3x the research quota across managed research services.
- **Private research graphs.** Sync durable claims, runs, evidence, and decisions that are visible only to owners and explicitly invited collaborators.
- **Synced service credentials.** Store supported service keys on your account and pull them to another signed-in machine.
- **Hosted researchers.** Ace and Ace+ include the same Synthetic Scientists access under the same concurrency policy.

## Link your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    ```bash
    openscience login
    ```
    Opens your browser to approve the device. On a headless machine, use `--no-browser`, or pass `--key thk_...` with a key created at `app.syntheticsciences.ai/cli`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    ```bash
    openscience status
    ```
    Shows the connected user, subscription, credit balance, research quota, synced services, and recent managed usage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync credentials">
    ```bash
    openscience sync
    ```
    Refreshes supported credentials after you change them in the Gateway.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Ace | $20 | **20 credits** | **Generous research quota** | Synthetic Scientists access and standard support |
| Ace+ | $100 | **150 credits** | **3x research quota** | The same Synthetic Scientists access, priority support, and early access |

Eligible `.edu` accounts receive 50% off the first month. Card processing is included in the plan price with no additional checkout fee.

Auto-reload is enabled by default for new Ace subscriptions. Checkout shows the reload amount, threshold, and monthly cap before payment. You can turn it off during checkout or anytime in Billing. Existing accounts are not enrolled without consent.

## Routing and billing

Routing and plan status are independent. An Ace subscriber may still use local, BYOK, or ChatGPT/Codex-backed models without a Gateway charge.

| Path | What runs where | Billing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local | OpenScience calls a local endpoint. | No Gateway charge. |
| BYOK | OpenScience calls your provider directly. | Your provider account; no Gateway charge. |
| ChatGPT/Codex | OpenScience uses the eligible signed-in route. | Your subscription; no Gateway charge. |
| Gateway managed | OpenScience calls the Gateway, which calls the provider. | Managed credits. |

Managed research queries are sent to the Gateway and its configured provider. If the Gateway is unavailable or a research quota is exhausted, OpenScience keeps non-managed routes working and explains the available fallback.

## Privacy

Content-free product telemetry is enabled by default and can be disabled in Settings. It records allowlisted operational events and aggregate usage, not prompt or response text, file content, notebook cells, or shell output. Research content is not collected or shared, and there is no content-sharing setting in this release.

## Account commands

| Command | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `openscience login` | Authenticate this device. |
| `openscience status` | Inspect connection, plan, credits, research quota, and recent usage. |
| `openscience wallet` | Inspect the managed balance and auto-reload state. |
| `openscience sync` | Refresh synced supported credentials. |
| `openscience devices` | List authenticated devices. |
| `openscience logout` | Disconnect this machine; local and BYOK routes keep working. |
