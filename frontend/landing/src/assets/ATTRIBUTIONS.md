# Landing-page photograph

`lunar-lab.jpg` is an archival photograph of chemist Sherwood Chang and
microbiologist Jo Ann Williams in the Lunar Chemical Laboratory at NASA Ames
Research Center, 1969. Photograph: **NASA/J. Remmington**.

- Source: [Scientists in the Lunar Chemistry Laboratory](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/scientists-lunar-chemistry-laboratory/)

- Usage guidance: [NASA images and media](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)

The file is the original black-and-white photograph, not an AI-generated image.
