# Connector logo attribution

These third-party marks are displayed only to identify the scientific sources available through OpenScience. Each mark remains the property of its respective owner, and its inclusion does not imply endorsement.

The official homepage and source URL for each displayed mark are recorded in [`src/data/connectors.ts`](../../src/data/connectors.ts).

## Official fallback marks

- **NCBI Gene** and **NCBI dbSNP:** their official pages render the database names as styled text and expose no distinct product image, so the shared NCBI favicon is retained.
- **SIFTS:** its official pages expose EMBL-EBI, PDBe, and UniProt marks but no standalone SIFTS image, so the shared EMBL-EBI favicon is retained.
